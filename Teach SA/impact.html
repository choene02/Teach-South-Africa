Impact

Since our inception in 2015, TEACH South Africa has made significant strides in transforming education across the nation. We have partnered with over 120 rural schools, trained more than 450 educators, and distributed upwards of 15,000 digital learning resources. Our impact extends beyond numbers; we have witnessed improved learner performance, increased teacher confidence, and enhanced community engagement. Join us in our mission to shape the future of education in South Africa.

Are you passionate about shaping the future of education in South Africa? Join us in our mission as a TEACH SA Ambassador!
