###  Teach South Africa Website Proposal

## 📘 Project Overview
This project outlines the design and development of a modern, responsive website for **Teach South Africa**, a non-profit organisation focused on improving access to quality education in underserved communities.

The website aims to enhance digital presence, provide educational resources, and support donor engagement through an interactive and user-friendly platform.

## 🏢 Organisation Overview

**Name:** Teach South Africa  

**Founded:** 2015  

**Description:**  
Teach South Africa is a non-profit organisation dedicated to addressing educational inequality by supporting learners and educators in rural and semi-urban communities.

**Mission:**  
To empower learners and educators through accessible, high-quality educational resources and training.

**Vision:**  
A future where every child in South Africa has equal access to quality education.

**Target Audience:**  
- Learners (Primary & Secondary)  
- Teachers  
- Parents  
- Education stakeholders
