Recommended security headers (configure in your hosting/CDN, not directly in static HTML):

Content-Security-Policy: default-src 'self'; img-src 'self' data: https:; style-src 'self' 'unsafe-inline' https:; script-src 'self' https: 'unsafe-inline'; connect-src 'self' https:; frame-src https:;
X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff
X-Frame-Options: SAMEORIGIN
Referrer-Policy: strict-origin-when-cross-origin
Permissions-Policy: geolocation=(), microphone=(), camera=()
Strict-Transport-Security: max-age=31536000; includeSubDomains

(If you deploy on HTTPS, ensure HSTS is enabled.)
